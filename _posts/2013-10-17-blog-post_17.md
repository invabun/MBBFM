<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">从收音机听到的一个广告，</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">当跨过难关后。</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">回首过去，</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">就像蚊子般的小事。</span><br />
<br style="color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; word-wrap: break-word;" />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">最近常被蚊子吵得睡不着...</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">简单的这一句话让我反思了许多东西...</span><br />
<br style="color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; word-wrap: break-word;" />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">1.如果照顾好环境不积水，蚊子也无处可长</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">酝意：每件事情都做好事前准备，那么问题来的时候也就迎刃而解。</span><br />
<br style="color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; word-wrap: break-word;" />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">2.被蚊子吵了，你忍受得了就无视它，不然就设法消灭它。</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">酝意：问题来了就解决它啊，觉得不重要的问题你就无视吧。</span><br />
<br style="color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px; margin: 0px; padding: 0px; word-wrap: break-word;" />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">3.用手打不到蚊子，可以点蚊香，蚊香赶不走可以用蚊油...甚至其他方法。</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">又何必在那里被蚊子烦着第二天早上起来说因为蚊子吵没睡好呢？设法消灭你才能安枕无忧啊。</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">酝意：解决问题有很多种方法，钻牛角尖或者逃避之前 吸一口气从更高的角度看待这件事。其实没什么好烦恼的。</span><br />
<span style="background-color: white; color: #31365f; font-family: Tahoma, Simsun, 'Microsoft Yahei'; font-size: 16px; line-height: 24px;">能消灭得了就不是问题，消灭不了的..那也没什么好烦恼的啦。</span><br />
