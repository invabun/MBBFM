<h3 style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<span style="font-size: small;">这是一个Dota世界中重大的日子，对与喜欢从未见过新事物的，现在带给你从原始基础地图环境的全面更新。同时伴随其它极大的其它变化，甚至包括Roshan从原始位置的离开。不过不用担心，他并没有走多远。它的位置更朝向河边，附近的道路也因为他的迁移而改观。虽然他的位置改变了，它更会使用相同的杀气能量来保卫它的领地。更多相关的变化可以透过战场去发现。<a name='more'></a></span></h3>

<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
It is a momentous day in the world of Dota, the likes of which have not been seen for ages, for now it is proven that even the land itself is not above being modified. Along with an assortment of other changes, it's moving day for Roshan. Though don't worry, he hasn't gone far. His pit now looks out upon the river from a different angle, and nearby paths have been rerouted around his legendary den. Though his location may have changed, you can count on him defending his territory with the same murderous energies. These changes, along with many more, can be found throughout the batlefield .</div>

<h2 style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
2.新赏金神符（Bounty Rune）</h2>
&nbsp;<a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiyP2989UnXgfPf_q8W7Jj4TxxcfnjEFR6YKnkBjpU1ZnMr12ZABx5MMA-4JpRGhdq3QGL1op5N0YWNJEVDEBQ1noHTnHOvhyzPHj8Z3EaVJNxkaDJRn38mkDrBBOFpj28T4ECMaXUKO_c/s1600/bounty_rune.png" imageanchor="1" style="clear: left; display: inline !important; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-right: 1em; text-align: center;"><img border="0" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiyP2989UnXgfPf_q8W7Jj4TxxcfnjEFR6YKnkBjpU1ZnMr12ZABx5MMA-4JpRGhdq3QGL1op5N0YWNJEVDEBQ1noHTnHOvhyzPHj8Z3EaVJNxkaDJRn38mkDrBBOFpj28T4ECMaXUKO_c/s1600/bounty_rune.png" height="328" width="400" /></a><br />
<b>这个符文的诞生是个不小的事件。这是锻造最根本的法宝，这种新的更新将在从来没见过的Dota世界中释放。赏金符文充满了金币和经验有助提高你的财富以及加速经验的累积。此外，符文现在每一次将同时在河的两端，其中一个一定会是赏金符文。有能力你就获取它加速累积你的能力吧！</b><br />
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<br /></div>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
The birth of a rune is no minor event. Forged of the most fundamental magic, this new update will unleash a rune never before seen in the world of Dota. Imbued with gold and experience, the Bounty Rune will serve to enhance your wealth and to speed the accumulation of abilities. Additionally, runes now spawn two at a time, with one at each end of the river. One of the runes will always be a Bounty Rune. Earn your bounty if you can!</div>

3.新道具：绯红卫甲（Crimson Guard）</h2>

<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<b>为了适应新的威胁，我们必须寻求新的防御。绯红卫甲（Crismson Guard）为了能在新盛起的混乱中对抗可怕的妖兽而产生的卫甲。穿上这个卫甲，你就可以准备召集你的军队并且带领抗敌。</b></div>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
To meet new threats, one must seek out new defenses. Originally forged during the chaos of New Bloom to defend against the dreaded Year Beast, the Crimson Guard is now ready to join the ancient war. Suit up in this armor, and you'll be ready to rally your forces and lead the charge against the enemy.</div>

<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<b>作为一个无眼的嗜血者尚未有足够让您恐惧，嗜血的变化更将把他推向另外一个境界。</b></div>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<b>新的技能(Blood Rite)已经替代屠戮(Bloodbath)，现在他更能将血液造成属于他的领域告诉你他一直看着你。</b></div>
<div>
<div>
As if an eyeless seeker of blood isn't frightening enough, perhaps Bloodseeker's changes will push him over the top. Along with a host of other changes, Bloodbath has been replaced by the new ability Blood Rite. You can now baptize an area in the Blood of the Twins, to show that they are always watching.</div>

Along with changes to his Juxtapose ability, he has earned a pair of new abilities: Doppelganger, which will help him dodge immediate danger and confuse enemies with illusions, and Phantom Rush, which allows Phantom Lancer and his illusions to swarm his foes.</div>
