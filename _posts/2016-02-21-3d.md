<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">喷头堵住了吐不出丝、 吐丝不顺畅断断续续的？</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">喷头堵塞原因总体可大体分为2类：1、耗材问题引起；2、操作不当引起；</span><br />
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">此处就此故障处理及日常维护作一个简明的说明。</span><br />
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"></span><br />
<a name='more'></a><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><br /></span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">1、PLA耗材由于温度过高碳化在喷头里面;</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">PLA耗材由于温度过高碳化在喷头里面形成黑色的粉末状固体。不能顺畅的随热熔态塑胶从喷嘴流出来。使用ABS耗材基本不存在此问题.</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">解决方法：将喷头导通后更换新的料，在下一次打印前先手动挤出一段料，将上一次的余料清理干净，在打印时注意适当降低温度</span><br />
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">2</span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">日常使用时操作不当引起；</span><br />
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">因个人在更换料时，不正规操作，导致进料齿轮或进料喉管至喷头处有残留的断丝，无法正常挤出，而堵塞喷头。</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">解决方法：先将残留的断丝清理出来，再重新放入耗材</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">3个人设置导致的;</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">打印速度设置过快,导致熔料跟不上,无法正常出料而堵塞喷头.这种情况会导致刚开始打印时很正常，但过十几分钟后堵头</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">解决方法：降低打印速度</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">4</span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">耗材本身问题引起；</span><br />
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">由于各不同厂商耗材质量参差不齐，或由于耗材加热后拉丝不均，或凝固异常，或含有杂质等问题引起喷头堵塞。目前市场上白色耗材普遍较其它颜色更易堵塞。</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">解决方法：换一种耗材</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">5打印机用一段时间后，里面会有氧化物，然后堵在打印头里面，这个需要定期清理。如果一天打印，堵头好几次，那么，肯定是线材的问题了。</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">也有可能是打印头疏通的没有彻底</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"></span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">喷头堵塞后，常见解决方法：</span></span><br />
<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">1</span></span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">、<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">&nbsp;</span>将喷头温度手动调节至<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">240</span>℃，PLA调至210℃，温度达到后，手动往进料喉管中挤压胶丝，使喷嘴正常出丝。接着停止加温，等温度降至<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">ABS150</span>度，<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">PLA110</span>度时拔出胶丝，使半熔化的胶丝将喷嘴中的残留物带出。反复操作几次。</span><br />
<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">2</span></span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">、<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">&nbsp;</span>使用<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">0.3mm</span>钢丝从喷嘴下方疏通喷嘴。然后重复方法</span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">1</span><span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">日常使用注意事项：</span></span><br />
<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">1</span></span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">在更换料时，取出材料时建议先启动进料程序，使喷嘴正常出丝，使喷嘴正常喷胶丝，使进料管中的材料连续性较好，然后再选择取出胶丝程序。这样有利于胶丝正常取出。</span><br />
<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">2</span></span><span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">在进行更换材料时，不可过于大力量往喷嘴填充胶丝，或者取出材料是不可过于大力量往外拉拔胶丝，这样都有可能拉断胶丝而残留在喷嘴或进料通道中而引起堵塞。</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">3</span>、加热喷头的发热管的固定功率是<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">30W.</span>如果将打印速度设置的非常高<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">,</span>进丝速度会相应的提高，也就是说要求加热到熔融状态的材料量增加，有可能超过发热管的极限。导致有些料熔化的不彻底。解决办法是：降低打印速度。</span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;PLA:</span>打印速度推荐：<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">40-50</span>；温度设置<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">200-230</span></span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;ABS:</span>打印速度推荐：<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">45-55;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span>温度设置<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">220-240</span></span></div>
<div align="left" style="background-color: white; color: #444444; font-family: 'Microsoft yahei', 微软雅黑, Arial, 宋体; font-size: 14px; line-height: 21px; outline: none; word-break: break-all; word-wrap: break-word;">
<span style="color: black; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;"><span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">避免堵头的最好方式就是<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">:&nbsp;</span></span><span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">使用<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">PL</span><span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">A</span></span><span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">材料时需养成清理喷头的良好习惯<span style="font-family: &quot;calibri&quot;; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">.</span></span><span style="font-weight: 700; outline: none; word-wrap: break-word;">每一次打印完成后都将喷嘴内的余料清理干净，每一次刚开始打印前都手动挤出一分钟料</span></span></div>
