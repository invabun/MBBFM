<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">要抽时间坐在电脑前面回忆其实也不是那么容易的。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">现在有种明白那些小说家的感觉哈。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"></span></span><br />
<a name='more'></a><span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第二坑 电脑城 ，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">不知不觉，在网吧呆了2年。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">这种时间真的非常恐怖。我从住在楼梯底到新店顶楼的破房，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">怎么破法？ 下雨漏水，洗澡没顶的。住在里面的我和两年前一样，只有一个包几件衣服。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">一个薄薄地毯，用衣服叠起来做的枕头。唯一不同的是 心里踏实了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">至少每个月都有省下钱，储蓄了一笔小钱。然而这样距离财务自还真的是很远。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我已经开始安耐不住自己</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">刚好一个朋友就说要去LowYat 开摊位卖电脑问我有没有兴趣。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我算了算，想了想该离开这个安全区了。 就这么层层3</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">开租一个摊位需要demo机，不然怎么给人展示对不对？</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">对 就那样一起出钱买了几台笔记本。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">有四个合伙人，租了一个摊位，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">位子很漂亮哦！LowYat 3楼电梯上来第一间。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">租金一个月12千！不是店铺 而是一个4个人站刚刚好的摊位。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">所以租金是3千块一个人。一台电脑可以赚RM500 一个月卖6台就够交租金了...</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">先不管这个价格是否市价。我在里面还是熬了3个月。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">对只有短短的3个月。怎么就那么短？</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">现在的人或许已经听过乔布斯说要最求自己最想做的事情。当年乔帮主还没那么出名啊。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">在Lowyat我们都变成了看到人就抓，张口就想吃人的怪物。那种感觉很悲哀。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">可我不那么拉客 连租金都交不起。心里悲哀。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而只熬了3个月的原因不是多辛苦，不是多高大上的我有远大的目标。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">而是一个幕后推手。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">可能精明的你已经觉得租金很奇怪怎么那么贵。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我先说说4个合伙人，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">两个呢 我还是比较相熟的，网吧常客。就叫AB君吧。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">另外一个是其中一个熟客认识十几年的朋友。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我对他一点都不认识。他一出来就是开Smart for 4。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">对当年的我来说这可是算是土豪了吧。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">叫做S先生好了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">开始租摊位的时候，我还以为Lowyat那么好赚 连S先生这种人都愿意去做这种销售。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">他给我们训练销售，背稿子背电脑spec, 背配套，配套送越多东西我们就赚越多，兼职比传销还传销。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">这没问题我虽然排斥还算是克服自己去做。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而奇怪的事情来了。一星期以后S先生再也没来销售。反而是请了一个员工过来。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">S先生说的员工是代替他在这里位子。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">问题来了，这位员工是拿工资+抽成的。 我们是交租金的养自己的。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">本来算是4人一体的分成方式忽然变成了竞争状况。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我感觉疑惑，他不应该做没有利益的买卖啊。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">可能是第一坑的时候说太多，这个时候我已经比较寡言。对自己说话没什么信心了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">而AB君一口同声的信任S先生。他们相识多么久了。我也没什么好说。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">而3个月傻傻的过去了。S先生说，楼下有个摊位只要10千我们搬下去可以省一点租金。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">这一说AB君也开始感到了什么。跑去问了管理层的人。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">从一开始都是S君谈的。AB君没有询问过，间接也让我安心觉得这人信得过。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而这回一问 AB君同时疯了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">原来12千租金的摊位 是10千 他每个月多拿2千白拿了3个月。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">让我们搬的是6千 他打算每个月白拿我们4千。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">相对我觉得我还比较冷静。AB君可是承受着背叛的痛。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我损失的是金钱和时间，他们是被背叛。那种愤怒憋屈不是我这个貌似局外人的家伙能感受得了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">瞬间拆伙是肯定的。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我们得到的只有所剩的一些Demo电脑。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">最后 我只比第一坑好一些。至少还有一点钱不至于太可悲。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">只是一切又从零起了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我也深深理解到 我真心不喜欢Lowyat那种环境。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">就再也不回头了的离开了那栋建筑物。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">依靠着网吧里和网游玩家接触拥相对比较了解的状况。看到一家网游公司在招客服。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">对这个行业感觉比较熟悉我就那么去面试且连续又做了两年，离开后做了几个月环保，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">又跑到另外一家网游公司做了另外2年。从客服到产品虽然也不是多开心。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">但很平稳的度过了这样的4年。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第一个坑 我在盲目情况下坑了自己以及身边的所有能坑人的人。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第二个坑 我看着别人被别人坑自己也陪葬了。我默默的人为那是一个报应。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">可我学到了教科书上不会有的人性。第一坑 说白了就是贪，第二坑说更清楚就是自己没有搞明白。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">巴菲特：“我只做我完全明白的事。”</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">这句话是我第三坑以后的日子才看到的。瞬间醍醐灌顶。我不明白 却没搞明白。只能说自己蠢。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">故事就说到这里</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第三坑再找时间写好了。</span></span>
