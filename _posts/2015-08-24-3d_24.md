<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">

<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
耗材的好坏存在以下几个主要质量问题。<br />
<br /></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
一、 原料材质质量；流动性和粘度是两个最主要的指标；其次是原料在加工过程中是否发生部分化学变化。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
二、 耗材精度。主要是耗材是否能控制在公差范围内。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
三、 耗材是否含有杂质。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
用户不是专业的所以辨别耗材主要从以下几个方面来观察。<br />
<br />
<a name='more'></a><br /></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
<b>一、 肉眼观察。</b></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
1.1请打开密封，仔细观察耗材是否存在色差；</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
1.2仔细观察，现在内部是否存在有微小的气泡；</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
1.3耗材如果是透明的，请仔细观察材质的色泽是否均匀；<br />
一般情况下色泽不均匀，说明耗材在生产时就发生了部分变化。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
1.4线材是否有黑色或其他颜色的斑点。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
肉眼观察是最为基础，掌握基础后小编建议大家学习高级技能 了解精度的原因后即使同样的3D打印机也可以大大提高精度问题<br />
<br /></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
<b>二、 精度。</b></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
2.1手感测试精度。拉开1-2米长的耗材，用大拇指和食指轻轻的夹住耗材，然后慢慢地去拉动耗材。如果耗材存在粗细不均匀或耗材表面不光滑，手感是很容易感知到的。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
2.2仪器测试。采用数显的游标卡尺来测量，是否在控制的公差范围内。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
2.3测量方式。在1-2米的长度内测量，测试线径的公差是否均匀；在每个测试点，旋转一周测量3-4个圆周的范围，主要测量线材是否“圆”，即：线材直径是否控制有效。<br />
<br /></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
<b>三、 打印观察耗材质量。</b></div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
3.1观察基板打印线条的均匀度。一般的打印机在打印开始都要做基板，观察喷头在基板上的均匀程度。一般情况下，比较均匀，耗材精度控制在范围内。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
3.2听声音判断。如果耗材在正常运行中，齿轮进料的装置，如果耗材不均匀，那么会发出“吭吭”的声音。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
3.3打印判断耗材的质量。观察耗材在打印中内部结构均匀程度。是否存在气泡、斑点等问题。</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
3.4粘度判断。耗材在打印过程中必须有粘度，否则打印就会开裂或脱离现象。考虑到耗材种类很多，各种打印机的基材都不同，材料会存在不相容的现象。为保证打印顺畅，最好在基板上涂上一些胶水。否则，由于打印机与模型在打印过程中的摩擦、碰撞等造成打印中断</div>
<div style="background-color: #fbfbfb; color: #313131; font-family: 'Microsoft YaHei', 微软雅黑, 黑体; line-height: 34px; padding: 0px; text-align: justify; text-indent: 32px;">
