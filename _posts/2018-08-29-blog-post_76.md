<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">明天就可以拿自己第一間房子的鑰匙，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">回想著10多年前的事。心情是百般感慨。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">活到今天，我跳過3次大坑（至少对我来说是大的）。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"></span></span><br />
<a name='more'></a><span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第一坑 傳銷。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">那年決定離開傳銷，一分钱都没剩下，落魄的回到出租屋。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">过两天就要交租金了，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">晚的晚餐都没着落，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">把房子行李收拾准备随时被赶出房子。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而发现抽屉和衣服堆里有一堆零钱，算了算7块3左右。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">自己在學院讀平面設計也沒畢業，能幹什麼呢？</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">想了一会，先活下去再說。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">抖了抖腳，整理了一下自己就出门，在最靠近的楼下每一间店面一家一家问工作。我已經沒錢為我爸借我的老爺車添油了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">用剩下的零钱买了5包泡面。問到了3份工作需要人，一份餐厅，一份网吧，一家五金店。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我也不是鐵人，安排了一下上班时间拒绝了五金店，因为餐厅包吃，网吧包住。人需要生存不就是吃和住麼。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">問我為什麼不和家裡借錢？我家裡也沒錢，加上我骨子里就是傲。借錢做傳銷的時候已經鬧翻了。朋友？也沒有了。年輕感覺面子很重要。自己有手有腳窮也不會餓死。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">就那樣我住进了网吧的储藏室。楼梯下面的那种。和服务器路由器一起睡觉。當年沒有拍照手機，不然我肯定要打印出來告誡自己。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">第一個月是最痛苦的沒出糧，沒有錢只剩下5包泡麵，我覺得我當時還是很幸運的，餐廳上班 到晚上10點，每次都吃炒麵炒果條 而且每次都會有剩下還可以打包。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">晚上吃了，早上中午加熱繼續吃。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">那五包泡麵呢？當時我覺得那是世界上最好吃的食物。我是當獎勵食用的。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">時光回退兩年，我在學院第一次接觸到傳銷。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">瘋狂的就All in去做了2年，賺過錢 一個月10多千</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">拽過， 傲嬌過，不過問題來了。有些事是你不加入你不會想到的。因為傳銷組織很善於讓人把重點集中在消費。在外表。 環境所驅加上自己年輕愚蠢，沒存款。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">對了不是做大組織嗎？能做多大？</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">來我告訴你一個我決定離開當天才搞懂的一個事實。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">如果你做傳銷 找5人 你下線找五人13輪后你就做不下去了。為什麼？那就已經超過地球人口了啊！</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">所以？秘密就在這裡， 你加入會員時這是盲區。我接觸所有人幾乎沒有人和我道破這點，全都被後面的$$給吸引了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而這個還是有一個很好的破解法，就是naruto 啊</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">複製自己，自己投資自己的account ,堆積一堆產品</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然後？找同樣的“夥伴”做同樣的事情啊。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">如果一個會員賬號是100塊 你就會付600 1+5嘛</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">甚至有更恐怖的 1+5+25 之類。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">反正目的就是和你越說越大。讓人的貪念打過理性。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">嘿我就是那個笨蛋好嘛。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">我就是 1+5 而且我的會員費還是2345的呢，</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">他們還會鼓勵購買一些無法使用 沒有價值的貴價產品。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">不能退貨。說是可以收藏。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">天真如我。還真做了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">經過2年full time我借的錢是還了，可是我對不起我組里的人，我對不起被我說服的人。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">然而我離開不久就聽到，我這一個金字塔頂尖 帶著一群人去開新的公司了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">回到兩年后，在網吧里。心裡的空虛，我因為環境因素沉迷網遊，水滸傳。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">幸運的是，在網遊其實很麻煩，網吧也很多麻煩讓人頭痛額事。然而在哪裡我真的學會了上網，上論壇找攻略，找怎麼修bug, directx 什麼的。我還真的需要謝謝網吧老闆。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">雖然工資市價就是那麼低，他讓哦賣網卡。我可以說是賺不少快了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">甚至搞笑的發生一件事，我在口香糖鐵盒子放入了一千塊紙幣 然後拿不出來。當然最后還是破壞鐵盒拿出來了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">反正我人生蠢事做真不少。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">兩年后，我就那樣帶著一筆小存款準備去跳第二個坑了。</span></span><br />
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;"><br /></span></span>
<span style="color: white; font-family: &quot;helvetica&quot; , &quot;arial&quot; , sans-serif;"><span style="font-size: 15px;">今天寫夠長了。真還有人有興趣 我再寫第二個坑吧。</span></span>
