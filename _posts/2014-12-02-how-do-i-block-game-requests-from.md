<h2 class="_2s4h" style="font-size: 20px; margin: 0px 0px 0.5em; padding: 0px;">
How do I turn off apps, games, plugins and websites?</h2>
<div class="_if" style="font-size: 13px; line-height: 19.9939994812012px; margin-bottom: 0.5em;">
<div class="_3p8" style="margin-bottom: 1em;">
To turn off apps, games, plugins and websites:</div>
<ol class="_341 _3p6" style="margin: 1.25em 2.4em; padding: 0px;">
<li style="padding: 0.35em 0px;">Click&nbsp;<img alt="" class="_254 img" src="https://fbcdn-dragon-a.akamaihd.net/hphotos-ak-xpa1/t39.2365-6/851557_364200877036449_574807949_n.gif" style="border: 0px; display: inline; margin: 0px 3px; max-width: 100%; vertical-align: middle;" />&nbsp;at the top right of any Facebook page (ex: your homepage)</li>
<li style="padding: 0.35em 0px;">Select&nbsp;<b>Settings</b></li>
<li style="padding: 0.35em 0px;">Select&nbsp;<b>Apps</b>&nbsp;in the left column</li>
<li style="padding: 0.35em 0px;">Under&nbsp;<b>Apps, Websites and Plugins</b>, click&nbsp;<b>Edit</b>&nbsp;and then&nbsp;<b>Disable Platform</b></li>
</ol>
<div class="_3p8" style="margin-bottom: 1em;">
Note: If you turn Platform off you can't use the Facebook integrations on third-party apps or websites. You may also lose information about apps or games (ex: favorite places, high scores). This info can't be recovered. Learn more about&nbsp;<a href="https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/your-info-on-other" style="color: #3b5998; cursor: pointer; text-decoration: none;" target="blank">sharing with other websites and applications</a>.</div>
